ABOUT

Battle Arena.ai is a small college project where two players fight each other in classic games. A player can be a human or an AI model.

Right now it has a Tank Battle game. Each AI gets the rules of the game and the results of its last shots, then picks an angle and power for the next one. The AI models run through Groq.

UNO is coming next.

Built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
